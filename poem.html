Small Things on the Way Home

The afternoon light falls across my notebook.
I close it before every question has an answer.
Outside, the buses arrive and leave,
and I walk past Rutgers buildings
with one unfinished thought still following me.

What I carry

  • a notebook with more questions than answers
  • a song I cannot stop humming
  • a little patience for tomorrow

Before I reach home

  1. Let the crowded bus go.
  2. Take the longer path beside the trees.
  3. Notice the sky before checking my phone.

The day does not end all at once.
It becomes footsteps,   then quiet.
For a moment, I have nowhere else to be.
