RESULT 02 // CONVERSATION ERASED

The argument is gone.

The sadness is gone too.

But then the good memories begin to disappear.

The jokes. The late-night talks. The feeling of being understood.

You also forget what the conflict taught you about listening and respecting other people.

You wanted to erase one wound.

Instead, you erased an entire chapter.
